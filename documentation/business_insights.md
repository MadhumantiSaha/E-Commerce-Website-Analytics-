# Business Insights

Six key findings derived from the Ecommerce Data Analysis Dashboard.  
These insights cover **marketing efficiency**, **refund risk**, and **customer retention** as required by the practical test (A6).

---

### 1. Marketing Efficiency – Paid Search has very low ROAS

**Paid Search** drives solid order volume but delivers a weak **ROAS of approximately 0.33**.  
It consumes the largest marketing budget while contributing only moderate revenue.

In contrast, the **Direct** channel achieves a strong ROAS (~3.6) with significantly lower spend.

**Recommendation**: Reallocate budget from Paid Search toward higher-ROAS channels (Direct and Organic Search) or apply stricter campaign controls and targeting.

---

### 2. Refund Risk – Email and Paid Search show elevated refund rates

Overall refund rate sits at a moderate **~5.6%**.  
However, two channels stand out:

| Channel       | Approx. Refund Rate |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Email         | ~7.8%               |
| Paid Search   | ~7.2%               |

Categories with relatively higher refund pressure include Fashion, Beauty, and Sports.

**Recommendation**: Improve product descriptions, imagery, and post-purchase communication for these channels/categories to reduce refund leakage and protect Adjusted Revenue.

---

### 3. Customer Retention is exceptionally strong

Out of all customers with valid orders, **~95% are repeat customers** (placed 2 or more orders).  
A large portion of the base has placed 4+ orders.

**Recommendation**: Shift focus from pure acquisition toward increasing Average Order Value and cross-selling / up-selling to the existing loyal customer base.

---

### 4. Electronics dominates both Revenue and Profit

The **Electronics** category is the clear profit engine:

- Highest Net Revenue contribution
- Highest Gross Profit
- Healthy margin (~31%)

Top products (Action Camera, Gaming Mouse, Mechanical Keyboard, Tablet Mini) drive a large share of overall profit.

**Recommendation**: Protect inventory, marketing focus, and assortment depth in Electronics.

---

### 5. Books & Learning has the best margin but low scale

**Books & Learning** delivers the highest Gross Margin (~46%) yet contributes the least revenue.

**Recommendation**: Explore controlled volume growth (bundles, targeted promotions, or cross-sell with higher-volume categories) without eroding the attractive margin.

---

### 6. Faster delivery reduces refund risk

Orders delivered in **0–3 days** show a noticeably lower refund rate (~3.5%) compared with deliveries in the 4–6 day window (~6.8%).

**Recommendation**: Treat logistics speed as both a customer-experience and a revenue-protection lever. Improving the slower delivery buckets can directly reduce refunds.
